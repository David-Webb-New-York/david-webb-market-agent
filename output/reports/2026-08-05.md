The 5 August 2026 scan captured **88 David Webb pieces** across six categories, of which **64 carry disclosed pricing** (58 dealer asking prices, 24 auction hammer results, and 6 estimates). The overall priced range spans **$2,500 to $215,000**, with a median of **$25,230** — a notable compression from yesterday's median of $34,700, attributable to a significant influx of mid-market inventory (total volume rose 57% day-over-day from 56 to 88 pieces). Necklaces and bracelets dominate the upper end of the price distribution, while earrings and rings cluster in the mid-tier. Animal-motif and enamel pieces continue to command premium asking prices, consistent with collector demand for Webb's most iconic design vocabulary.

## Data-Quality Caveats

- **Asking prices are retail-resale comparables** sourced from dealer platforms (primarily 1stDibs); they represent list prices, not confirmed transaction values, and are subject to negotiation.
- **Auction hammer prices exclude buyer's premium.** Sotheby's standard buyer's premium (as of 2026) adds approximately 25–26% to the hammer for lots in this price range; total acquisition costs are materially higher than hammer figures shown.
- **Occasional miscategorization** is possible where dealers list convertible pieces (e.g., brooch/pendant, bracelet/cuff) under a single category; the Maltese Cross duplicate listing is a specific flag for this scan.
- **The "Other" category** contains only 2 priced items; its median of $48,125 and max of $93,750 are not statistically representative and should not be used for benchmarking.
- **Historical hammer dates vary**; auction results sourced from Sotheby's may reflect sales from different years (e.g., the 2014 lot visible in one URL), and are included as directional comps rather than current-market equivalents.
