## Data-Quality Caveats

- **Auction hammer prices exclude buyer's premium.** The total cost to the buyer (with premium) is typically 15–26% higher depending on the house and lot tier.
- **Multi-currency records** (ITL, HKD) appear in the auction dataset at their original sold currency. Where USD equivalents appear in category statistics, conversion rates applied at time of sale may differ from current rates.
- **Dealer asking prices are not confirmed sale prices.** They represent the dealer's current ask and may be subject to negotiation or may sit unsold indefinitely.
- **"Delisted" status** for dealer items means the listing is no longer visible in the active inventory feed. This *usually* indicates a sale but can also reflect a voluntary withdrawal, a price relist under a new SKU, or a data-feed gap.
- **Earrings minimum ($1):** The $1 floor in the dealer earrings category almost certainly reflects a data-entry placeholder or a listing error and should not be taken as a genuine market price.
- **This is the baseline run.** All "new this week" counts reflect the initial backfill, not genuine weekly inflow. Week-over-week comparisons will be meaningful beginning with the report dated 2026-08-14.
